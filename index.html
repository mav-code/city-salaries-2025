First steps into NYC's best-paid public jobs

Each card is one way to start a career with the City of New York: a trade, a civil-service exam, a degree, an appointment or an election. Together the 84 cards cover all 443 job titles that paid someone at least $200,000 in the city's official 2025 payroll. Each card shows the best-paid job its start led to, that job's 2025 pay, and the postings that set out the requirements.

Pay is total fiscal-year 2025 pay (July 2024 – June 2025): regular pay plus overtime plus other pay such as lump sums and retroactive raises. Base salary is shown beside it; a large gap usually means a one-off payout. Base salary is the annual rate at the end of the fiscal year, so it can be higher than what was actually paid when someone got a raise or started partway through the year.

Source: NYC Office of Payroll Administration, Citywide Payroll Data (Fiscal Year). Titles share a card when their entry path is the same; appointed jobs are grouped by who appoints. A title counts once it paid at least one person $200,000 or more in total. To look up individual employees, the same payroll is searchable at SeeThroughNY.

Not affiliated with the City of New York. Requirements change; check the current exam notice or posting before applying.
